Hidden Toggle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the Hidden Toggle publishing tool (the "App"), which Hidden Toggle uses to upload its own videos to its own social media accounts.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of Hidden Toggle, to manage Hidden Toggle's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It isn't available to the public and doesn't sign in or act on behalf of any other person.

What data the App accesses

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok for Hidden Toggle's own accounts, used only to upload videos and read basic account details such as the channel name.
  • Hidden Toggle's own video files, titles, captions and hashtags.

The App doesn't collect, store or process personal data about viewers or any other users. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens are stored privately by the account owner and used only to call the platforms' official APIs. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than publishing Hidden Toggle's videos.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Instagram and TikTok

Use of Instagram and TikTok through the App follows Meta's Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from each platform's account settings for connected apps.

Deleting data

Because the App holds no data about anyone other than Hidden Toggle, there is nothing to delete for other users. The owner can remove all stored tokens at any time by revoking the App's access on each platform.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Hidden Toggle through a direct message on its YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account.
